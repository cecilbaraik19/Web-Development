# Sports Complex and Gym Rules

The new Sports Complex behind Block D opened on 1 October 2026. It has a gym, an indoor badminton hall and a yoga room. There is no swimming pool on campus.

## Gym timings
| Day | Time |
|---|---|
| Monday to Saturday | 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM and 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM |
| Sunday | 7:00 AM to 11:00 AM |
Girls-only gym hours are 4:30 PM to 5:30 PM on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

## Gym membership
The gym is free for hostel students. Day scholars pay ₹500 per semester. Register at the sports office with your ID card and a fitness form signed by a doctor.

## Rules
- Wear sports shoes and carry a small towel.
- Use each machine for at most 20 minutes when others are waiting.
- The trainer, Mr. Sunil Kujur, is present in all gym hours. Ask him before using heavy weights.
