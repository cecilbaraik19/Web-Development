# Central Library Rules

The Central Library is on the first floor of the Academic Block B. It has more than 42,000 books, 60 print journals and a digital section with 40 computers.

## Library timings
| Day | Time |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM |
| Saturday | 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM |
| Sunday and holidays | Closed |
During end-semester exams the reading room stays open until 10:00 PM, all seven days.

## Borrowing books
| Member | Books at a time | Loan period |
|---|---|---|
| Undergraduate student | 4 books | 14 days |
| Postgraduate student | 6 books | 21 days |
| Faculty | 10 books | 90 days |
| Non-teaching staff | 2 books | 14 days |
The library ID is the college ID card. Books can be renewed twice on the library portal if no one else has reserved them. Reference books, journals, thesis copies and newspapers cannot be taken out of the library.

## Overdue fine
A fine of ₹2 per day per book is charged for the first 7 days after the due date, and ₹5 per day after that. Fines are paid at the circulation counter or on the student portal. A student with an unpaid fine cannot borrow new books.

## Lost or damaged books
If a book is lost, the student must replace it with a new copy of the same edition or pay double the price of the book. A damaged book is treated as a lost book.

## Digital library
Students can use NPTEL video courses, DELNET, IEEE and Springer e-journals, and the e-book collection from home through the remote login link on the library portal. The username is the college email ID. Ask at the help desk if the login does not work.

## Rules inside the library
- Keep silence. Group discussion is allowed only in the two discussion rooms, which can be booked for 2 hours at the counter.
- Bags must be left at the property counter.
- Food and drinks are not allowed. Water bottles are allowed.
- Photocopying is available at ₹1 per page; only up to 10% of a book may be copied.

## No-dues certificate
In the final semester, every student needs a library no-dues certificate. Return all books and pay any fines; the certificate is issued on the same day.
