# Faculty Leave and Duty Policy

This policy is for teaching staff only.

## Types of leave
| Leave | Days per year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Casual leave | 12 days | not more than 3 days at a time |
| Earned leave | 15 days | can be carried forward up to 180 days |
| Medical leave | 10 days | doctor's certificate needed for more than 2 days |
| Duty leave | as approved | for conferences, exam duty at other universities, FDPs |
| Maternity leave | 180 days | as per government rules |
| Paternity leave | 15 days | within 3 months of the child's birth |

## How to apply
Apply for leave on the HR portal at least 3 working days before (except medical and emergency leave). The HoD approves leave of up to 3 days; longer leave also needs the Principal's approval. Before going on leave, the faculty member must arrange a substitute for classes or reschedule them, and record this on the portal.

## Exam duty
Every faculty member must do invigilation duty as given by the Examination Cell. Exchanging duty is allowed only with another faculty member and must be reported to the Examination Cell one day before. Missing exam duty without information is treated as leave without pay.

## Professional development
Each faculty member gets a professional development allowance of ₹15,000 per year for conferences, certifications and workshops. Unused amount does not carry forward. Faculty must attend at least one Faculty Development Programme (FDP) every year.

## Working hours
Faculty working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Faculty must mark attendance with the biometric system at arrival and departure.
