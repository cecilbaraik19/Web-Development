# Student Clubs and Events

Clubs are run by students with a faculty coordinator. Joining a club is free. Club registrations open in the second week of August on the student portal, and the "Club Fair" is held in the central lawn in the third week of August.

## Technical clubs
- ByteBuilders (coding club): weekly coding contests every Thursday at 5:00 PM in Lab B-204, and a 24-hour hackathon in September. Coordinator: Mr. Abhishek Tigga.
- CipherSquad (cyber security club): capture-the-flag (CTF) practice every Saturday, bug bounty sessions and talks on ethical hacking. Members must sign the club's ethical-use pledge; testing any system without written permission is not allowed. Coordinator: Ms. Shalini Barla.
- RoboVerse (robotics club): builds robots for inter-college competitions; works in the Innovation Lab, C-012.
- Data Circle: data science and AI study group with Kaggle challenges.

## Cultural and other clubs
- Nritya (dance), Swarang (music), Rangmanch (drama) and Kalam (literary and debate).
- Eco Club: tree plantation and campus clean-up drives.
- NSS (National Service Scheme): a student needs 120 hours of service over two years to get the NSS certificate.
- NCC: one army wing company; enrolment in August for first-year students.

## Sanskriti — the annual fest
Sanskriti is the annual tech and culture fest, held for three days in February. It has a hackathon, a CTF contest, robo-race, dance and music competitions and a food fair. Students of other colleges can take part by registering online. Volunteers get duty leave for the three days.

## Starting a new club
At least 15 students and one faculty coordinator are needed to start a new club. Send the proposal to the Dean of Student Welfare with a list of planned activities. New clubs are approved in September.

## Event permission
Any club event on campus needs permission from the Dean of Student Welfare at least 7 days before. Events must end by 8:00 PM. Outside speakers need approval from the Principal.
