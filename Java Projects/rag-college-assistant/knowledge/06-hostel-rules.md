# Hostel Rules and Fees

SVIT has two hostels on campus: Birsa Bhawan for boys (220 beds) and Kanchan Bhawan for girls (180 beds). Rooms are given on a first-come, first-served basis, and first-year students get priority.

## Hostel fees
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Room rent, twin sharing | ₹48,000 per year |
| Room rent, single room | ₹60,000 per year |
| Mess charges | ₹3,600 per month |
| Security deposit (refundable) | ₹10,000, once |
| Electricity | as per sub-meter reading, billed each semester |
The same fees apply to the boys' and girls' hostels. Room rent is paid once a year in July; mess charges are paid every three months in advance.

## In-time and night out
The in-time is 9:00 PM for Birsa Bhawan and 8:30 PM for Kanchan Bhawan. Attendance is taken in the hostel at in-time.
To stay out at night or go home, fill the night-out form on the hostel portal at least 24 hours before. A parent must confirm by phone or by replying to the SMS. Students who are late more than three times in a month are reported to their parents and the Chief Warden.

## Mess timings
| Meal | Time |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM |
| Lunch | 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM |
| Evening snacks | 5:00 PM to 5:45 PM |
| Dinner | 8:00 PM to 9:30 PM |
On Sundays breakfast is served from 8:00 AM to 9:30 AM. The mess committee, with two student members from each hostel, plans the weekly menu. Students who are away for at least 7 days in a row can claim a mess rebate if they inform the warden before leaving.

## Visitors
Parents and guardians on the visitor list can meet students in the visitors' room from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM on Saturdays and Sundays. Visitors are not allowed in the rooms. Overnight guests are not allowed.

## Not allowed in the hostel
- cooking appliances, heaters and electric kettles
- alcohol, tobacco and drugs (this leads to expulsion from the hostel)
- pets
- making noise after 10:30 PM
- changing rooms without the warden's permission
Damage to hostel property is charged to the student, and to all room-mates when the person cannot be found.

## Wi-Fi and laundry
Hostel Wi-Fi is available from 6:00 AM to 12:00 midnight. Laundry service is available at ₹600 per month (optional).

## Leaving the hostel
To leave the hostel, apply to the warden at least 15 days before. The security deposit is refunded after the room check and hostel clearance, within 30 days.
