# Student Handbook 2026-27

This handbook gives the main rules for every student of Sarna Valley Institute of Technology (SVIT). Read it together with the Examination Regulations and the Fee Structure notice.

## College timings
Classes run from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Saturday. The second and fourth Saturdays of every month are holidays. The lunch break is from 12:50 PM to 1:30 PM. The main gate closes at 6:30 PM for day scholars.

## Attendance
Every student must have at least 75% attendance in each subject, counted separately for theory and practical classes.
Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may be allowed to sit the end-semester exam only on medical grounds. They must submit a medical certificate to the HoD within 7 days of returning to college. This is called condonation, and a fee of ₹500 per subject is charged.
Students below 65% attendance in a subject are detained in that subject and must repeat it with the next batch.
Attendance is updated on the student portal every Friday. If your attendance is wrong, report it to the subject teacher within one week.

## Leave
For leave of up to 3 days, write an application to your class mentor. For more than 3 days, the application must be approved by the HoD. Medical leave needs a doctor's certificate. Leave taken for college events (sports, fests, hackathons) is marked as duty leave and counts as present, if the event coordinator sends the list to the department within 3 days.

## Identity card
Every student gets a photo identity card at admission. Wear the ID card on campus at all times; security staff may stop students without it. A lost ID card must be reported to the Student Section. A duplicate card costs ₹200 and is ready in 3 working days.

## Dress code
Students should come in neat, decent clothes. Formal dress (shirt, trousers or salwar suit, and closed shoes) is required on Mondays, during lab exams, seminars, and on placement days. Shorts and slippers are not allowed in classrooms or labs.

## Mobile phones
Mobile phones must be switched off or on silent in classrooms, labs and the library. A phone used during class can be taken away by the teacher and returned only by the HoD at the end of the day.

## Certificates from the Student Section
The Student Section (Room A-102) issues bonafide certificates, character certificates, migration certificates and transcripts.
- Bonafide certificate: apply on the student portal; ready in 2 working days; free of cost.
- Character certificate: ready in 3 working days; free of cost.
- Transcript: ₹300 per copy; ready in 7 working days.
- Migration certificate: ₹500; issued only after the final semester and no-dues clearance.
The Student Section is open from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on working days.

## Mentoring
Every student is given a faculty mentor in the first semester. Mentors meet their group of about 20 students once a month. Students can talk to their mentor about studies, attendance, stress or personal problems. The college counsellor is also available (see Offices and Contacts).
