# Fee Structure 2026-27

This notice gives the fees for the academic year 2026-27. It replaces the Fee Structure 2025-26.

## Tuition fee per semester
| Programme | Tuition fee per semester | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| B.Sc. IT | ₹38,000 | 3 years (6 semesters) |
| BCA | ₹42,000 | 3 years (6 semesters) |
| B.Tech CSE | ₹68,500 | 4 years (8 semesters) |
| B.Com (Hons) | ₹24,000 | 3 years (6 semesters) |
| MCA | ₹55,000 | 2 years (4 semesters) |

## Other fees
- Examination fee: ₹1,500 per semester, paid together with the tuition fee.
- Caution deposit: ₹5,000, paid once at admission. It is returned after the course ends, after no-dues clearance.
- Development fee: ₹3,000 per year, paid in the odd semester.
- Hostel and bus fees are separate. See the Hostel Rules and the Transport notice.

## Due dates
The odd-semester fee must be paid by 31 July 2026. The even-semester fee must be paid by 15 January 2027.

## Late fee
A late fee of ₹50 per day is charged after the due date, up to a maximum of ₹1,500. If the fee is not paid within 30 days after the due date, the student's name is struck off the rolls and the student cannot sit the semester exam. Re-admission after that needs the Principal's approval and a re-admission fee of ₹2,000.

## How to pay
Fees can be paid on the student portal by UPI, net banking or debit card. A demand draft in favour of "SVIT Fee Account" is also accepted at the Accounts Office. Cash is accepted only up to ₹10,000. Keep the receipt; the portal also lets you download it.
Students can pay the tuition fee in two instalments if they apply to the Accounts Officer before the due date: 60% by the due date and 40% within 45 days.

## Refund of fees
If a student cancels admission:
- before classes start: full refund minus a processing fee of ₹1,000;
- within 15 days after classes start: 80% of the tuition fee is refunded;
- between 16 and 30 days after classes start: 50% is refunded;
- after 30 days: no refund of tuition fee.
The caution deposit is always refunded. Refunds are paid to the bank account within 30 days of approval.

## Accounts Office
The Accounts Office (Room A-110) is open from 10:00 AM to 3:30 PM on working days. Email: accounts@svit.example.in.
