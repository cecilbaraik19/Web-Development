# Examination Regulations

These rules apply to all undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. The Controller of Examinations runs the Examination Cell.

## Marks scheme
Each theory subject has 100 marks: 30 marks internal assessment and 70 marks end-semester exam. Internal assessment is made of two mid-semester tests (10 marks each), assignments (5 marks) and attendance and class work (5 marks).
Each practical subject has 50 marks: 20 internal and 30 in the end-semester practical exam.

## Passing rules
To pass a subject, a student needs at least 40% of the total marks and at least 35% in the end-semester exam. A student who fails a subject has a backlog in it and must clear it in the supplementary exam.

## Eligibility to sit the exam
A student can sit the end-semester exam only if the attendance is at least 75% (or condoned, see the Student Handbook), all fees are paid, and the internal assessment is complete.

## Admit card
Admit cards can be downloaded from the student portal 7 days before the exam. Carry the admit card and the college ID card to every exam. Without both, entry to the exam hall is not allowed.

## In the exam hall
- Report 30 minutes before the exam starts. No one is allowed in after the first 30 minutes of the exam.
- Students may not leave the hall during the first hour.
- Mobile phones, smart watches, earphones and programmable calculators are not allowed. Simple calculators are allowed only when the question paper says so.
- Write your roll number only on the cover page of the answer book.

## Unfair means
Copying, carrying notes, talking or using a phone in the exam is called unfair means. The penalty depends on the case: cancelling that paper, cancelling all papers of the semester, or debarring the student for one year. The Unfair Means Committee hears the student before deciding.

## Results and grades
Results are published on the portal within 45 days of the last exam. Grades and grade points are:
| Marks (%) | Grade | Grade point |
|---|---|---|
| 90 and above | O (Outstanding) | 10 |
| 80 to 89 | A+ (Excellent) | 9 |
| 70 to 79 | A (Very good) | 8 |
| 60 to 69 | B+ (Good) | 7 |
| 50 to 59 | B (Above average) | 6 |
| 45 to 49 | C (Average) | 5 |
| 40 to 44 | P (Pass) | 4 |
| Below 40 | F (Fail) | 0 |
SGPA is the credit-weighted average of grade points in one semester. CGPA is the same average over all semesters. To change CGPA into a percentage, multiply it by 9.5.

## Re-evaluation and photocopy
A student who is not happy with the marks can apply for re-evaluation within 7 days of the result. The fee is ₹750 per paper. A photocopy of the answer book costs ₹500 per paper. If the marks change by more than 10%, the re-evaluation fee is returned. Re-evaluation results come within 30 days. Apply on the student portal under "Exams".

## Supplementary exams
Supplementary (backlog) exams are held twice a year, in June and in December. The fee is ₹1,000 per paper. Register on the portal at least 20 days before the exam. The internal marks of the first attempt are carried forward.
