# Code of Conduct, Anti-Ragging and Grievances

## Anti-ragging
Ragging in any form is a criminal offence and is completely banned on campus, in hostels and in college buses. Ragging includes teasing, rude behaviour, forcing a student to do anything against their will, and any act that causes fear, shame or physical or mental harm.
Every student and parent must submit the anti-ragging undertaking online at the time of admission.
The Anti-Ragging Committee is headed by the Principal. An Anti-Ragging Squad visits the hostels, canteen and buses at random times.
To report ragging, call the college anti-ragging helpline 0651-2440011 (24 hours), write to antiragging@svit.example.in, or call the national anti-ragging helpline 1800-180-5522. The name of the person who complains is kept secret.
Punishment can be suspension from classes, cancellation of hostel seat, a fine of up to ₹25,000, expulsion from the college, and a police complaint.

## General conduct
- Smoking, alcohol and drugs are not allowed anywhere on campus.
- Political posters and banners are not allowed.
- Students must not damage college property. The cost of repair is charged to the student.
- Photos or videos of teachers or other students must not be taken or posted without their permission.
- Cases of misconduct are heard by the Disciplinary Committee, which can warn, fine, suspend or expel a student.

## Grievance redressal
Students can raise a complaint about academics, fees, hostel, facilities or behaviour of staff on the online grievance portal. Each grievance gets a ticket number. The Grievance Redressal Committee replies within 7 working days. If the student is not satisfied, they can appeal to the Principal within 15 days.

## Women's safety and Internal Complaints Committee
The Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) handles complaints of sexual harassment. It is chaired by Prof. Rekha Bara. Complaints can be made in writing or by email to icc@svit.example.in and are kept confidential. The Women's Cell also runs self-defence workshops every semester.

## Student council
Each class elects one representative in August. Class representatives elect the Student Council, which works with the Dean of Student Welfare to organise events and raise student issues.
