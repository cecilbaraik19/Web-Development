# B.Sc. IT Programme Structure and Syllabus

The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (B.Sc. IT) is a 3-year programme with 6 semesters and 132 credits. It is run by the Department of Information Technology. Head of Department: Dr. Meena Tirkey.

## Credits
Each semester has 20 to 24 credits. One credit means one hour of theory or two hours of lab work per week. A student must earn all 132 credits to get the degree. The degree must be completed within 5 years of admission.

## Operating Systems (ITC-301) units
- Unit 1: Introduction, types of operating systems, system calls.
- Unit 2: Processes, threads, CPU scheduling (FCFS, SJF, Round Robin, Priority).
- Unit 3: Process synchronisation, semaphores, deadlocks and the Banker's algorithm.
- Unit 4: Memory management, paging, segmentation, virtual memory and page replacement.
- Unit 5: File systems, disk scheduling, and a case study of Linux.

## Semester 4 subjects
Computer Networks (ITC-401), Web Technologies (ITC-402), Software Engineering (ITC-403), Data Structures and Algorithms (ITC-404), Python Programming (ITC-405), and two labs.

## Electives in semester 5
Students choose two electives from: Cyber Security and Ethical Hacking, Cloud Computing, Data Science with Python, Mobile App Development, and Internet of Things. An elective runs only if at least 15 students choose it. Elective choices are made on the portal in the last week of the fourth semester.

## Major project in semester 6
In the sixth semester, students do a major project in groups of up to 3, under a faculty guide. The project carries 8 credits. There is a proposal in week 2, a mid-term review in week 8 and a final viva in week 15. Plagiarism above 20% in the report is not accepted.

## Lab rules
Students must bring the lab record book to every lab. Lab files must be signed by the teacher within one week of the experiment. Students must not install software on lab computers without permission.
