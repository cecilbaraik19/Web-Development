# Transport and Campus Facilities

## College buses
SVIT runs 9 buses on 6 routes. The bus fee is ₹9,000 per semester for any route. Apply for a bus pass on the student portal before classes start; the pass is ready in 3 working days. Students must show the bus pass when they board.
| Route | Main stops | First pickup |
|---|---|---|
| Route 1 | Railway Station, Main Road, Lalpur, Campus | 7:30 AM |
| Route 2 | Bus Stand, Kokar, Booty More, Campus | 7:35 AM |
| Route 3 | Harmu, Argora, Doranda, Campus | 7:25 AM |
| Route 4 | Hatia, Dhurwa, Sector 2, Campus | 7:20 AM |
| Route 5 | Kanke Road, Morabadi, Bariatu, Campus | 7:40 AM |
| Route 6 | Namkum, Tatisilwai, Campus | 7:15 AM |
All buses leave the campus at 4:45 PM. On exam days there is an extra trip at 1:30 PM. A lost bus pass can be replaced for ₹100.

## Canteen and food court
The main canteen is open from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM on working days. A meal costs ₹60 to ₹90, and tea or coffee costs ₹10. The food court near Block C has a juice bar and a fast-food counter, open from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## Sports
The campus has a cricket and football ground, two basketball courts, a volleyball court and an indoor hall for table tennis and badminton. The sports room issues equipment against the ID card from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM and 4:30 PM to 7:00 PM. The annual sports meet is held in December.

## Wi-Fi
Campus Wi-Fi ("SVIT-Campus") covers all academic blocks and the library. Register your device at the IT Help Desk first. Social media and streaming sites are blocked during class hours from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM.

## Other facilities
- ATM of a public sector bank near the main gate.
- Stationery and photocopy shop in Block A, open 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM.
- Parking: two-wheelers in the parking near Block D, with a parking sticker issued by the security office. Students may not park cars on campus.
- Drinking water with RO filters on every floor.
