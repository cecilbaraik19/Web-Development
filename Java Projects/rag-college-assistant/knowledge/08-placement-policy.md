# Training and Placement Policy

The Training and Placement (T&P) Cell invites companies for campus recruitment and helps students with internships, aptitude training and mock interviews.

## Who can register
Students in the final year of B.Sc. IT, BCA, B.Tech, B.Com and MCA can register for campus placement. To be eligible, a student needs:
- at least 60% marks in Class 10 and Class 12,
- a CGPA of at least 6.5 up to the last semester,
- no active backlog at the time of the drive.
Some companies set higher cut-offs; the cut-off in the company notice is final.
Registration is done on the placement portal in July. The one-time registration fee is ₹1,500.

## One student, one job
A student who gets an offer of ₹4 lakh per year (LPA) or more can only apply to "dream companies" after that. A dream company is one that offers at least 1.5 times the student's current offer. Students with an offer below ₹4 LPA can keep applying to all companies.

## Rules for drives
- Attending the pre-placement talk is compulsory for students who apply.
- Formal dress and a printed resume (2 copies) are needed on the day of the drive.
- A student who registers for a drive and does not appear without a valid reason is debarred from the next two drives.
- Accepting an offer and then refusing it later leads to removal from the placement process.

## Internship
A summer internship of 6 to 8 weeks is compulsory for B.Sc. IT and BCA students after the fourth semester. It carries 4 credits. The student submits a report and gives a presentation in the fifth semester. The internship can be at a company, a research lab or an approved online programme. Students must get the internship approved by the department internship coordinator before it starts.

## Training
The T&P Cell runs aptitude and soft-skills training every Wednesday and Saturday from the third semester. Mock interviews and group discussions are held in July and August for final-year students.

## Placement record 2025-26
In 2025-26, 64 companies visited the campus and made 142 offers. The highest package was ₹12.5 LPA and the average package was ₹4.2 LPA. Top recruiters were TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant and two cybersecurity start-ups.
