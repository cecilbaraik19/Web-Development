# Academic Calendar 2026-27

| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Classes start (semesters 3 and 5) | 21 July 2026 |
| Orientation for first-year students | 28 July to 31 July 2026 |
| Classes start (semester 1) | 3 August 2026 |
| Last date for odd-semester fee | 31 July 2026 |
| Independence Day (holiday) | 15 August 2026 |
| First mid-semester test | 14 September to 19 September 2026 |
| Durga Puja break | 17 October to 26 October 2026 |
| Second mid-semester test | 2 November to 6 November 2026 |
| Last teaching day | 13 November 2026 |
| End-semester practical exams | 16 November to 21 November 2026 |
| End-semester theory exams | 24 November to 10 December 2026 |
| Winter break | 21 December 2026 to 3 January 2027 |

|---|---|

| End-semester exams | 3 May to 22 May 2027 |
| Summer vacation | 1 June to 18 July 2027 |
| Supplementary exams | 14 June to 24 June 2027 |

## Notes
Results of the odd semester are expected by the third week of January 2027. Results of the even semester are expected by the first week of July 2027.
If the government declares an extra holiday, the class lost is made up on a Saturday announced by the Dean.
