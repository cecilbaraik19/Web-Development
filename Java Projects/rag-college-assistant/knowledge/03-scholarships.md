# Scholarships and Fee Concessions

The Scholarship Cell helps students get scholarships from the college and from the government.

## Merit scholarship (college)
The top three students of each programme and semester get a tuition fee waiver in the next semester:
| Rank | Waiver of tuition fee |
|---|---|
| First | 50% |
| Second | 30% |
| Third | 20% |
To keep the merit scholarship, the student needs an SGPA of at least 8.5, at least 75% attendance and no disciplinary case.

## Need-based freeship
Students whose family income is less than ₹2.5 lakh per year can apply for a freeship of up to 40% of the tuition fee. An income certificate issued by the government is needed. A committee checks the documents and may call the student for an interview.

## Government scholarships
Students from SC, ST, OBC and minority groups can apply for the state post-matric scholarship and the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) schemes. The college verifies the online form after the student submits it. The Scholarship Cell runs a help desk in the first two weeks of August.

## Other concessions
- Sports quota: students who represented the state or the university get a 25% tuition waiver.
- Sibling concession: if two siblings study at SVIT at the same time, the younger one gets a 10% tuition waiver.
- Only one college scholarship or concession can be taken at a time; the higher one is given.

## How and when to apply
Applications for college scholarships open on 1 August and close on 31 August every year. Apply on the student portal under "Scholarships".
Documents needed:
- last semester grade card
- income certificate (for freeship)
- caste certificate (for government schemes)
- Aadhaar-linked bank account details
- passport-size photo
Results are announced on the notice board and the portal by 30 September.
