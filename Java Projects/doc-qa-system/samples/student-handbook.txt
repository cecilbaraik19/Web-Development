Student Handbook 2026 — Sample Document

1. Attendance
Students must attend at least 75 percent of classes in every course.
Students below 75 percent attendance cannot sit the end-semester exam.
The Dean may relax this rule for documented medical reasons.

2. Library
The central library opens at 9 am and closes at 8 pm on weekdays.
On Saturdays it is open from 10 am to 4 pm. It is closed on Sundays.
Students may borrow up to five books for fourteen days.
A fine of 5 rupees per day is charged for late returns.

3. Fees
The semester fee must be paid before the 10th of the first month of the semester.
A late fee of 500 rupees is charged after that date.
Fees can be paid online through the student portal or at the accounts office.

4. Examinations
Mid-semester exams are held in the eighth week of the semester.
End-semester exams are held in the last two weeks of the semester.
Students must bring their ID card to every exam. Mobile phones are not allowed in the exam hall.

5. Hostel
Hostel gates close at 10 pm. Visitors are allowed only in the common room between 4 pm and 7 pm.
